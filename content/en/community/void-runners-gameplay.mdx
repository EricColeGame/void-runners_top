## What Void Runners Is, Per the Official Listing

The Steam page for [Void Runners](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5334520/) describes it as a co-op salvage raid for one to four pilots, playable on a single couch or online. The central mechanic is the beam: ships are linked in pairs by a beam that shreds anything it sweeps. That single sentence does most of the design work. You are not just flying a ship and shooting; you are managing a spatial relationship between two ships, because the space between them is a weapon.

The store description also confirms:

- Crossfire, dodging, and reviving crew from escape pods as part of the moment-to-moment loop
- A structure of three sectors and three guardians deep
- Building a salvaged ship as a progression concept
- One to four players, couch or online

That is close to the full extent of what is publicly documented. The page does not publish ship parts, weapon stats, boss names, unlock costs, or any code system. If a guide or video claims to list those things, treat it as speculation rather than developer documentation.

## Why the Beam Changes How You Play

Most twin-stick co-op games treat other players as parallel damage sources. Void Runners appears to invert that: your teammate is part of your weapon. The beam exists between ships, so its position is determined by two players' movement at once, not one.

That produces a few practical consequences worth reasoning through before launch:

**Splitting up is not automatically correct.** The listing mentions "spreading out and swinging the web," which suggests that widening the formation is a deliberate tool, not a default. Two ships far apart sweep a longer line; two ships close together sweep a shorter, denser one. The right answer depends on what is in front of you.

**Positioning is a shared decision, not an individual one.** If you fly aggressively while your partner retreats, you are stretching the beam rather than aiming it. Coordinated movement is the skill ceiling here, which is presumably why the game supports couch play, where voice chat is free.

**Crossfire implies angles matter.** The description names crossfire explicitly, which suggests overlapping beam lines are a real tactical option. That is a reasonable inference from the wording, but the specific damage behavior of overlapping beams is not documented.

The official description states a run goes three sectors and three guardians deep. However, the page does not specify how many jumps are in each sector, how long a sector takes, whether runs are timed, or how difficulty scales with player count.

Anything you read that assigns a specific number of jumps, boss names, or sector themes is not sourced from the developer. The reliable way to confirm run length is to play the game after release, or to check the official Steam page for updates between now and Q1 2027.
