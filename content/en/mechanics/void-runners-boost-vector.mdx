In Void Runners, the beam is the game. Aaron Reisman's twin-stick roguelike for one to four pilots does not ask you to aim a gun so much as to position a line: each pair of ships is joined by a tether that shreds whatever it sweeps, which means your movement is your damage. Understanding that one sentence is most of what separates a clean run from a pile of escape pods.

Everything below comes from the official store description, and where the page is silent, this article says so rather than guessing. You can read the source yourself on the [Void Runners Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5334520/), which lists the game as coming soon for Q1 2027.

## What the Beam Actually Is

According to the official description, "beams link ships that shred anything they sweep." That phrasing matters. The beam is not a projectile, not a cooldown ability, and not a toggle you fire. It is the geometry between two players. Wherever the segment connecting your ship to your crewmate passes, enemies in that path take damage.

Three consequences follow directly from that:

- **You cannot aim and move separately.** Your firing solution is your flight path. Turning is aiming.
- **Damage scales with cooperation, not with upgrades you have not seen.** A pair of ships sweeping a wide arc covers more ground than one ship dragging a beam behind it.
- **Two players means one beam.** Three players means three beam pairs. Four players means six. The official description does not state the exact pairing math, but it does say "each pair of ships is joined by a beam," so more pilots means a denser web.

The store page does not publish beam length, damage numbers, tick rates, or whether beams can be upgraded. Those details are not public yet, and you should verify them in-game after launch rather than trusting any figure you read elsewhere.

## Spreading Out and Swinging the Web

The official description names two behaviors explicitly: "spreading out and swinging the web." These are not decorative phrases. They describe the two competing pressures of the beam system.

**Spreading out** lengthens the beam, which widens the swept area but thins your coverage. If two ships fly to opposite edges of an arena, the beam crosses the middle and rarely touches the flanks. Enemies at the edges go untouched.

**Swinging the web** is what happens when the crew rotates around a shared center. If everyone drifts in the same rotational direction, the beams sweep like scythes and cover a huge footprint, at the cost of leaving the center of the formation unguarded.

The practical tension, then, is between area and density. A tight formation shreds anything that comes close. A wide formation shreds anything at range but lets enemies slip through the middle. The official page frames this as the core decision of flying relative to your crew, which is a design statement, not a tip.

## Crossfire, Dodging, and the Cost of Getting It Wrong

The store description also lists "crossfire" and "dodging" as advertised features. Read together with the beam mechanic, this implies the same lines that kill enemies can create danger for you: crossing beams with a teammate is how you build crossfire, and crossing them carelessly is how you get boxed in. The official page does not say whether friendly beams damage friendly ships, so treat that as undocumented until you can test it.

What the page does confirm is that death is not necessarily the end of a run. Crew can be "revived from escape pods," meaning a downed pilot leaves a pod that surviving teammates can recover. Whether that requires flying into the pod, holding position near it, or simply reaching it is not documented on the store page.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Void Runners the same as Void Crew or Void Stranger?**
No. It is a separate game developed and published by Aaron Reisman, listed under Steam app 5334520. It is not Solar Ash, Void Crew, Void Stranger, or Starfinder.

**How many players can join?**
One to four pilots, on one couch or online, per the official Steam description.

**Does the beam require a button press to fire?**
The official page does not describe an activation input. It describes beams that link ships and shred whatever they sweep, which reads as always-on geometry.

**How long is a run?**
Three sectors, four jumps each, ending at a guardian, with three guardians total across the run. Specific timings are not published.

**Where can I confirm details?**
The [official Steam listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5334520/) is the authority.
