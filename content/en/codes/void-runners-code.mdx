## What the Official Store Page Actually Confirms

The Steam listing is the authority here, and it is unusually specific about the game's core loop. Void Runners is described as a co-op salvage raid for one to four pilots, played on a single couch or online. Ships are linked in pairs by a beam, and that beam shreds anything it sweeps. The pitch is built around positioning rather than button mashing: spreading out and swinging the web of beams, catching enemies in crossfire, dodging what comes back at you, and reviving fallen crew from escape pods.

Structurally, the store description says a run crosses three sectors and ends at a guardian, with three guardians in total. There is also a mention of building a salvaged ship, which implies some form of progression between or during runs.

That is the full confirmed picture. Notably absent: ship parts, weapon stats, guardian names, sector layouts, unlock costs, hotkeys, and any code or redemption system. If a page claims to list those things today, it is guessing, aggregating rumors, or padding.
